01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Good planning is where function meets a clear vision.

I’m Amir Hassan, based in Islamabad, Pakistan. My work is centered on town planning and AutoCAD drafting, with an interest in creating layouts that make efficient use of land and residential spaces.

I approach each assignment by understanding the requirements first, organizing the information and then presenting the design through clear, practical drawings. I value accuracy, thoughtful circulation and a clean drawing standard.

Whether it’s a plot subdivision, a housing layout or a residential floor plan, my aim is to make the design easy to review and ready for the next project stage.
